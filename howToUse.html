~ How to use this site ~

(1) Type what you want to search in the search box.

(2) Press enter and search.

(3) Look for the video you want to watch.

(4) Click it! * It might not work first try.

(5) Click the "show search box" button to open search box.

(6) Click "language: English" and change the language used to search.

new!! (7) Put a url of a video and click open to directly watch the video.

* Be awear that the video owner can stop you from watching the video.
